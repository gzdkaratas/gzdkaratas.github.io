Research

Privacy-preserving, trustworthy, and interpretable machine learning.

Federated Learning and Machine Unlearning

Training models across distributed data sources without centralizing sensitive data, and removing the influence of specific data afterward. Work includes aggregation algorithms, differential privacy against inference attacks, SISA-based unlearning across tabular, visual, and audio data, and unlearning for biometric recognition.

Intrusion Detection and Security

Deep learning and ensemble methods for network intrusion detection, malware detection, and IoT attack detection, including SHAP-based interpretability analysis and federated approaches to security.

Healthcare and Applied AI

Clinical decision support and disease prediction with federated and ensemble methods, plus applied systems such as fraud detection agents and learning-to-rank for e-commerce search.
