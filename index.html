About

I am a computer engineer working on federated learning, machine unlearning, privacy-preserving and trustworthy machine learning, and cybersecurity applications of AI, with growing work in healthcare AI. I have supervised doctoral and master's students, led interdisciplinary research projects, secured competitive research funding, and taught AI, security, programming, and data science at undergraduate and graduate levels. I served as Vice Head of Department at two universities.
